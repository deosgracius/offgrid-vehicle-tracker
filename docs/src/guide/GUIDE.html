1  The Devices and Their Buttons

The radio (green box, antenna) does the talking. The display (dashboard screen) shows where your partner is. Four controls run everything.

ControlShort press / tapLong press / hold
Radio USERNext screen or menu lineOpen action menu; confirm selection
Radio POWER Switch the radio on
Display MAINNext pageReset tracking; on Messages page, clear history
Display BOOTRequest partner's position now 
Display RESETRestart the display (rarely needed) 
THE ONE RULE. Never run the radio's battery flat. A dead battery can erase its settings, and the radios stop understanding each other until a laptop fixes it.

2  Powering On

1Hold the radio's power button until the screen lights. This home screen is normal. Indoors, "0 online" and "No Sats" are not faults: satellites need sky, and your partner may be off.

2Power the display at the same time, like an ignition key. The radio accepts the display only during its first minute, so always switch them on together. Missed it? Power both off and on again.

3Watch the page fill in. "waiting for GPS fix" clears under open sky; "PTR not heard yet" clears when your partner's radio is first heard. Normally live within two minutes.

3  The Two-Second Pre-Flight Check

Before every trip, short-press to this LoRa screen on both radios and read one number: Freq: 912.875 MHz. Both right: everything will work. Either one different: that radio lost its settings and needs the Technical Manual's recovery before the trip.

While you are here, the rest of the screen should read Role: Client Mute and US/LongFast, exactly as shown.

Short presses walk a loop of status screens.

Clock. Set by the GPS satellites; nothing to adjust. A correct clock proves the GPS has spoken to this radio.

5  Sending a Message

Ready-made messages, so nobody types while driving. Short press moves, long press chooses.

1Long-press on the chat screen; choose Reply.

2Destination: #Group9, the channel both vehicles hear. Skip Free Text.

3Scroll the 21 presets (Help!, Send position, On my way, Turn left, Stop...). Long-press to send.

Replying: long-press, Reply to Last Msg, With Preset. Every question in the list has its answer in the list.

6  The Display While You Drive

Hands off: it updates itself twice a second. Tap cycles three pages.

Messages. Received texts, newest first. Hold here to clear the history.

Partner's position now: press BOOT. The age drops to seconds.

Proven on the road: the screen read 700 ft at 10 o'clock, the driver turned, and the partner's car appeared in the windshield. Trust the clock directions.

8  How Far It Reaches

Measured on real roads in October 2026, with the antennas inside the cars. The honest rule from all our testing: distance is not what breaks the link, blocked line of sight is.

Where you areWhat to expect
In town, buildings aroundReliable to about half a mile. Buildings eat radio signal.
Open road, flat countryFully reliable to 3.26 miles in our test. The screens went stale by 3.75 miles.
Canyon or hill countryThe STALE banner comes and goes at any distance, even close together, whenever rock sits between the cars. In our canyon test it appeared inside 1.3 miles, yet updates still slipped through past 4 miles when the land opened up.
Whenever the data is staleThe display shows DATA STALE and the age counts up. Nothing is broken, and no radio can beat rock. Keep driving toward the last known direction; the moment the two cars can "see" each other again it heals by itself, no buttons needed.
WANT MORE RANGE? Mounting the antenna on the roof instead of inside the car can roughly double the distance. Ask the team before changing hardware.

9  Menus to Leave Alone

DO NOT TOUCH LORA ACTIONS. Device Role, Radio Preset, Frequency Slot and LoRa Region are the exact settings that let the two radios hear each other. Changing any of them silences the pair with no error shown. Same for Bluetooth Toggle under System Action: switching it off strands your display.

10  Powering Off and Charging

Charge over USB-C after every trip. Switched off, the radio holds charge for months. Never store it empty.

11  When Something Looks Wrong

12  Restoring a Radio That Forgot Its Settings

A radio that runs its battery completely flat can lose part of its configuration when it next starts. Nothing is broken and nothing needs re-flashing; the settings only have to be put back. You will know it has happened when one or more of these appear:

SignWhat was lost
LoRa screen reads 906.875 instead of 912.875The Group9 channel (name and key), so the radio is on the public frequency and cannot hear the partner.
Home screen shows "Meshtastic_ead4" or "Meshtastic_bb5c" instead of SF or RGThe owner name.
Display stays on SEARCHING or CONNECTING for minutesBluetooth pairing mode went back to "Random PIN", which the display cannot use.
Partner position updates only every few minutes, or jumps on a coarse gridPosition timing and precision.
The preset message list is the factory oneThe canned messages (Section 5).

The losses come in batches, so always restore everything, not just the one thing you noticed. There are two ways to do it. The phone way needs no laptop and works at the roadside; the laptop way is faster and restores every setting at once.

12.1 With a phone, no laptop: about five minutes

You need: the Meshtastic app (free, Android or iOS) and the Group9 channel QR code. Every team member keeps the QR code in their phone's photos; it is also in the team's private hardware notes. The QR code contains the channel key: never post it, print it, or send it outside the team.

  1. Switch on the radio by itself. Leave the display off until the end.
  2. In the app, open the connection screen (Bluetooth) and tap the radio. It appears as Meshtastic_ead4 (the SF radio) or Meshtastic_bb5c (the RG radio), or as SF or RG if the name survived. If the phone asks for a PIN, read the six digits from the radio's own screen and type them in.
  3. Restore the channel first. Open the Channels screen, choose the scan-QR option, and scan the Group9 QR code. Accept it. The radio reboots. Check the radio's LoRa screen: it must now read 912.875. This one step brings back the channel name, the key, the frequency, and full-precision positions.
  4. Bluetooth pairing mode. Reconnect if the app dropped the radio, then open Radio configuration, Bluetooth, and set Pairing mode to No PIN. Save; the radio reboots again. Until this is done the display cannot pair.
  5. Name. Radio configuration, User: long name and short name both SF (or both RG).
  6. Role. Radio configuration, Device: Role Client Mute.
  7. Position timing. Radio configuration, Position: GPS update interval 10 s; position broadcast interval 60 s; smart position on, minimum interval 10 s, minimum distance 20 m.
  8. Presets, optional. Module configuration, Canned message: enabled, with the preset list from Section 5. Typed messages work without this step; only the preset list is missing.
  9. Disconnect the phone from the radio in the app (the radio accepts one Bluetooth connection at a time). Now power the display: it should show the link within a minute.
In a hurry: steps 3 and 4 are what the partner radio and the display need. Everything else can wait until you are parked.

12.2 With the laptop: about ninety seconds

Each radio has a saved snapshot of its complete, correct configuration: sf-config.yaml for the SF radio and rg-config.yaml for the RG radio, kept with the team's private hardware notes because they contain the channel key. Plug in the radio alone by USB, with the display unplugged so the radio's port is the only one, and run:

python -m meshtastic --configure sf-config.yaml

Use rg-config.yaml for the RG radio. The radio reboots once or twice while the settings are written. Then check the radio's own screens: the LoRa screen reads 912.875 and the home screen shows SF (or RG). If anything still reads wrong, run the same command once more; a setting that makes the radio reboot occasionally misses the first pass. If the laptop has more than one serial device attached, add --port COMx with the radio's port.

After any deliberate settings change, take a fresh snapshot so the saved file stays correct:

python -m meshtastic --export-config > sf-config.yaml

12.3 Afterwards

Switch both radios on outdoors. Within a minute, each radio's Last Heard screen should show the other, and the display should show the link. Then do the pre-flight check of Section 3 before driving. To make this section unnecessary: charge after every trip and never store a radio empty (Section 10).
